FAQ

Short answers.

How quickly is my shop connected?
With the plug-in, under ten minutes: install, enter your API key, done. From then on, orders appear as shipments in the dashboard automatically. Custom systems get a REST API with a sandbox — most teams go live in an afternoon.
Is there a base fee or a contract term?
No. You pay per shipment, billed monthly on one consolidated invoice. No minimum volume, no term, no notice period. Ship more and you get better rates — automatically.
How does carrier selection work?
NEOS routes every shipment to the right partner based on destination zone, weight, transit-time target and price. You can set rules (say, “France always via Colissimo” or “express above €50 order value”) or simply let the automation run.
How do returns work?
Your customers use a returns portal in your branding and receive a label from the local carrier. The return travels back through the same network and appears in the dashboard with status and cost — at the same reduced rates.
What about customs for destinations outside the EU?
For Switzerland, the United Kingdom and Norway, NEOS generates customs documents automatically from your order data (goods description, HS code, value). You choose DDP or DAP per destination; the carrier handles clearance.
How do my customers see tracking?
Through a NEOS tracking link that looks the same for every carrier and country. Optionally in your branding, with status emails or SMS in German, English, French, Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Polish.
Do I need an account?
No. Enter the address and parcel details, pay, and the label arrives by email. If you ship often, you can create an account to save addresses — entirely optional.
How can I pay?
Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay or instant bank transfer. The price shown is the final price incl. 19% VAT — nothing is added at checkout.
Where do I drop off my parcel?
At any parcel shop or locker of the chosen carrier — we show you the nearest ones at checkout. Or add collection at your door and pick a time slot.
What can I put in the parcel?
Anything the carrier transports to the destination country: no dangerous goods, no cash, no perishables. We check weight and dimensions at checkout; for destinations outside the EU we fill in the customs documents together with you.
What if something breaks or gets lost?
Every shipment is covered up to €50 of goods value; you can add extra cover for higher values at checkout. Report a claim via the tracking link — we take care of the rest.
Do I get an invoice showing VAT?
Yes, automatically by email right after payment, with 19% VAT itemised. If you ship for a company, switch to the “Business” tab: there you see net prices and volume rates.
